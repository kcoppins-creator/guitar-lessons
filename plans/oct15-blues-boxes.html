Five Blues Boxes + Hesitation Blues

Thu Oct 22 · 6:00–6:30 PM ET 30 min Blues foundations

Coach: Boxes are partial — connect them like CAGED highways, not five islands (you self-rated CAGED-connected at 3/5; same disease). Tonight also drills 7ths on I7–IV7–V7 and the major-3rd vs blue-3rd clash (awareness, not perfection). Hesitation only works if Time Guru proves you’re late on purpose — speaker/wired, not AirPods.

1. Setup

  • Tuning: Standard. Key for drills: A minor pent / A blues (5th fret box 1).
  • Optional slide nearby for Hesitation flavor — not required tonight.
Say out loud: “Five boxes = one blues map. Box 1 at fret 5 in A; connect to box 2 before licks. A7 = A–C♯–E–G (1–M3–5–b7).”
Pocket cue (Time Guru): Today’s rung = 2 and 4 (use gap mute if you want a harder check). ~60 BPM — speaker or wired headphones, not AirPods. Groove target: one Hesitation phrase (setup on time; last note a fraction late). If everything is early, it’s not hesitation — it’s rushing. Foot tap quarters. Voice Memo + listen once (~6–8 min).

2. Key & context

Drill key: A blues. A minor pentatonic = A C D E G; blues adds D♯/E♭ (♭5).

Hesitation Blues: traditionally A or E country-blues feel — use lesson key; map boxes to that tonic.

GPS / map: Five boxes aren’t five countries — they’re five neighborhoods on one city map. The root note is the same address appearing on different streets. Tonight find the shared alley between box 1 and 2.

Traffic lights + toolbox: I7 is a green that still wants to move (b7). V7 is red. The pentatonic C (b3) rubbing on A7’s C♯ (M3) is two wrenches that almost fit — that rub is the blues sound. Slide C→C♯ to choose.

5. Exercises

  1. Box 1 ascending/descending ×4 with Time Guru at 60 (2 & 4). Say “root” every time you hit A.
  2. Box 1→2 bridge ×6: play 3 notes in 1, 3 in 2, return. No pause at the seam.
  3. 7th spell ×1 each: Say A7 / D7 / E7 as 1–3–5–b7 out loud (notes).
  4. I7–IV7–V7 targeting: Over A7 land C or C♯ deliberately (name which); over D7 land C or A; over E7 land G♯ or D.
  5. Hesitation: Learn 1 phrase from lesson; leave a “hesitation” rest before the resolution — late is the point.
