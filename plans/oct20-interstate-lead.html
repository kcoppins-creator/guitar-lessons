Interstate — chords to solo targets

Tue Oct 27 · 6:00–6:30 PM ET 30 min STP lead

Coach: Oct 15 built the map; tonight you drive it. Goal from placement: connect scales to chord changes. One scale forever = sightseeing without stops. Chord tones on the change = GPS recalculating each turn. Re-lock C♯m = C♯–E–G♯.

1. Setup

  • Standard tuning. Reuse Oct 15 (Interstate) C♯m/E map. Light drive.
  • Optional: loop chorus changes on phone for the 12-min drill.
Say out loud: “Same vi–I–IV world. Solo = chord tones on changes, not one scale forever. Over C♯m I land E (m3) or G♯; over E I land G♯ (M3) or B.”
Pocket cue: Land the target tone on beat 1 of each new chord. If you’re late, cut the previous lick shorter — don’t rush the landing to “catch up.”

2. Key & context

Recall: C♯m / E. Tonight convert Oct 15 Interstate harmony into lead landings.

GPS turn-by-turn: C♯ minor pentatonic is the highway. At each chord change, exit to the chord-tone offramp (3rd or 5th), then you can merge back onto the highway. Miss the exit = you “sort of” sound right; hit the exit = you sound intentional.

5. Exercises

  1. C♯m spell lock ×2: “C♯–E–G♯ — m3 quality.”
  2. 3rds only ×4 through C♯m–E–A–E (E, G♯, C♯, G♯ respectively). Say “3” on each land.
  3. 1–5 only ×2 same loop — balance the 3rds work.
  4. Approach notes: one scale tone into each 3rd (below or above).
  5. Phrase with change: start lick on C♯m, land G♯ or B on beat 1 of E.
  6. Nearest-inversion lead ×4: play triad grips as double-stops into single-note targets.
  7. Play lesson hook excerpt half speed if needed — mark chord tones in the hook.
Toolbox: Pentatonic = full drawer. Tonight you may only use the M3/m3 wrench on each chord for a full pass. Restriction builds the skill you asked for: theory connected to changes.
