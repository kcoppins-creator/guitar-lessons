2. Key & context

Guitar-relative (common): C major family. With capo 5, concert F, etc. Buckley popular version frequently taught with capo + C shapes.

Signature: walk C – Am – C – Am – F – G – C – G – C – F – G – Am – F – G – E – Am (approx. — verify with lesson).
