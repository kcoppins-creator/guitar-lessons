Six Ways — Musical Alphabet / fretboard

Thu Nov 12 · 6:00–6:30 PM ET 30 min Fretboard lab

1. Setup

  • Standard. No song — pure map. Pick string set: start on low E and A.
Say out loud: “Musical alphabet is A–G with sharps/flats. Every fret is a letter — I’ll say them ascending and descending.”

2. Key & context

No concert ‘song key’ — drill in C major pitch names (no sharps in scale) then add accidentals on chromatic pass.

5. Exercises

  1. E string chromatic ×2 saying letters.
  2. A string ×2.
  3. Skip-fret quiz: partner-less — random fret, name note in 2s.
  4. Octave hunt: play all E notes in first 12 frets.
