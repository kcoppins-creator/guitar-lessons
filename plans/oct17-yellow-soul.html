2. Key & context

Yellow Ledbetter: Concert E major. Progression mostly E – B – A – E (I–V–IV–I). Hendrix-thumb voicings up the neck.

Soul to Squeeze peek: Follow the verified lesson’s capo/key; spell Romans before groove. Full deep session Nov 7.

GPS / map: Same Hendrix “thumb chord” shape sliding up and down the neck is one vehicle; frets 7 / 5 / open neighborhoods are different exits on the same highway (V, IV, I colors).

5. Exercises

  1. Shape cycle ×8: E–B–A–E whole notes with thumb bass. Speak Romans I–V–IV–I.
  2. Interval pass ×2: On each chord name the 3rd quality (M3 for all three majors here).
  3. Embellish E: Hammer 9→11 on G string while holding double-stops (Wind Cries Mary DNA). Then rest one beat.
  4. Target pass: Solo only roots, then only 3rds (G♯ / D♯ / C♯), then only 5ths over the loop. Say the degree as you play.
  5. Nearest-inversion fill (5 min): On strings 2–3–4, walk E→B→A with common tones — furniture rule.
  6. Soul peek (20 min): Learn chorus chords only; say Romans; one clean pass. Write 1–3–5 for each chorus chord in your notes.
Toolbox: E major pentatonic is the full drawer. Tonight’s solo drill uses only the three wrenches that match the chord under your fingers — that’s why McCready sounds “in” even when sparse.
