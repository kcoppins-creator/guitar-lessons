2. Key & context

Concert key: Centered on C♯ minor / E major (relative pair). No capo — guitar pitch = concert pitch.

Core idea: Intro/chorus loop often C♯m – E – A – G♯ – A – E. Verse adds walkdowns and tension (G♯/C, A♯dim, Asus2, E–Esus colors). Tonight prioritize functions, not every fill.

Two cities, one metro: C♯m and E major share all the same notes (relative). Starting on C♯m is living on the minor side of town; the chorus takes the train to the E major downtown. Same metro map — different stop called “home.”

Traffic lights: C♯m = soft home. E = bright green. A = yellow (leaving). G♯7 = red flash — it wants to resolve (often back toward C♯m or through A). Don’t park on the red light.

Light 7ths (song supports this)

When you grab G♯7, spell 1–3–5–b7 = G♯–B♯–D♯–F♯. The b7 (F♯) is the “guest chair” that leans toward resolution — triad is the 3-person table; the 7th invites the next chord in.

5. Exercises

  1. C♯m spell lock ×4: Hold C♯m; arpeggiate; say “C♯, E, G♯ — root, m3, P5.” Wrong answer from intake = C–E–G♯ (don’t go there).
  2. Say-and-strum ×4: C♯m–E–A–E. On each chord speak Roman (vi–I–IV–I) and 1–3–5.
  3. Add III color ×4: C♯m–E–A–G♯–A–E. Notice G♯ wanting to pull — say “V of vi” or “III color” once each pass.
  4. Triads strings 2–3–4 ×6: Nearest inversions only through C♯m→E→A→E. Furniture rule: don’t lift fingers that can stay.
  5. E–Esus–E pocket ×4: Sus then resolve to M3 (G♯). Feel tension→release as yellow→green.
